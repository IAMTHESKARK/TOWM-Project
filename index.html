About the Book

"The Other Wes Moore" by Wes Moore tells the story of two young African-American men who shared the same name and similar backgrounds but ended up on vastly different paths. This powerful narrative explores the factors that influenced their divergent destinies.

Wes Moore (Author)

Rhodes Scholar, paratrooper, White House Fellow, and bestselling author.

Wes Moore (The Other)

Currently serving a life sentence for the murder of a police officer.
